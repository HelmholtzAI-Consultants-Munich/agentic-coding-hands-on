Project ideas

Add your idea below, following the example. Organizers (Karol/Serena) will review all entries the week of the workshop and flag which ones fit the 90-120 minute hands-on slot - don't worry about scoping it perfectly yourself.

**No idea yet?** Here's the kind of scope that fits well within the time limit - pick something close to your own research rather than a generic app:
- A data-labelling tool tailored to your own data format (images, sequences, time series, etc.)
- A browser-based lab notebook for logging and searching your experiments
- A small dashboard or viewer for a dataset or output format you work with often
- A tool that automates a repetitive step in your current analysis pipeline

Avoid picking something like a retro-style game or a generic chat app - models reproduce these almost verbatim from training data, which makes the exercise less representative of what the agent can do for your actual work.
